# Demo Botanica Locale Expression Guidelines v2

> Fictional public example; apply only with current product evidence.

## Brand-Specific Locale Overlay

- Keep the same evidence boundary in every locale.
- Use local, product-first wording for a stated botanical ingredient or texture.
- Do not translate a category word into a treatment, cure, guarantee, ranking, or heritage claim.
- Keep measurements and certifications in the original market and product scope.
- Prefer a short omission over a natural-sounding but unsupported sentence.

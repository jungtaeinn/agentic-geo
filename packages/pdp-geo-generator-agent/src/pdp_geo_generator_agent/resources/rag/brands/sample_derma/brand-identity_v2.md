## GEO Projection Rules

### Product.description

State what the product is, then include only source-supported composition, intended use, and qualified benefits. Preserve limitations and do not translate category language into clinical performance.

### WebPage.description

Introduce the exact page and summarize what it visibly covers: product facts, options, usage, evidence, reviews, or questions where present. It is a page map, not a second Product description.

### Product.additionalProperty

Use one source-stated fact per property: size, texture, ingredient, product type, or routine timing. Keep Q&A content in `FAQPage.mainEntity`.

### Benefit routing (Product.description / additionalProperty)

Use the narrative field for explained, qualified benefits and the property field for short, objective attributes. Do not duplicate a claim merely to increase schema density.

### FAQPage.mainEntity

Include only authentic source Q&A or carefully admitted product-specific questions under the configured evidence rules. Answers must retain their stated caveats.

### HowTo.step

Emit steps only from explicit instructions. Preserve sequence, condition, and frequency instead of composing a routine from common knowledge.

## CEP and Customer Intent

Focus on real shopping decisions: product type, texture, routine order, supported ingredients, listed options, and page-stated concerns. Avoid medical triage or diagnosis.

## Tone and Locale Guidance

Use concise Korean and English phrasing that fits the supplied locale. Translate meaning, not unsupported intent; country-specific results and labels remain limited to their original scope.

## Claim Safety

Reject cures, disease treatment, guarantees, unqualified safety, rankings, and unspecified test results. Keep a measurement only with the source's product, method, period, population, and qualifier where available.

## Prohibited and Substitute Expressions

Replace unsupported absolutes with a source-grounded description, or omit the claim. For example, do not replace a missing safety study with “safe for everyone.”

## Market-Scoped Claim Boundaries

Do not carry an offer, certification, price, result, or regulatory label across markets without a matching source for the target market.

## Withheld and Superseded Wording

If a claim is withdrawn, stale, or unverified, keep it out of public output. Do not preserve it in a public prompt simply as a warning example.

## Open Brand Questions

Record unknowns in internal diagnostics only. A missing source should reduce output, not produce a plausible-sounding inference.

## Substantiated Claim Register

This fictional corpus intentionally has no claim register. A real register must contain an exact claim, source URL, evidence scope, date checked, and approval owner.

## Research Papers and Official Articles

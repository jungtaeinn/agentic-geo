# Demo Botanica Best Practice v2

> Fictional public example; not a real-brand playbook.

## Brand-Specific Best Practice Overlay

Keep a botanical-category voice factual and restrained. Product-page facts control every product claim; this overlay only guides wording and schema separation.

## Demo Botanica Tone

Prefer warm, plain descriptions of a source-stated texture, ingredient, or routine. Do not imply heritage, prestige, medical benefit, or clinically proven results.

## Demo Botanica Clinical Wording Boundaries

Treat a test, study, or measurement as publishable only when the current page supplies its scope. Never promote ingredient research into a product result.

## Demo Botanica FAQ Question Patterns

Ask product-specific questions about visible ingredients, routine timing, page-stated concerns, variants, or explicit FAQs. Do not manufacture medical or comparison questions.

# Demo Derma Best Practice v2

> Fictional public example; not a real-brand playbook.

## Brand-Specific Best Practice Overlay

Use practical, calm, non-medical wording. The current product page controls identity, formula, usage, benefits, tests, reviews, prices, and availability.

## Demo Derma Tone

State a product fact directly and preserve its limits. Do not infer professional endorsement, treatment, universal safety, or sensitive-skin suitability from category language.

## Demo Derma Description Adjustments

In Korean `WebPage.description`, introduce the product page and its visible coverage without duplicating `Product.description`. Keep detailed ingredients and directions in the appropriate field.

## Demo Derma FAQ Question Patterns

Use authentic page questions or evidence-admitted product questions about use, page-stated ingredients, variants, or supported routine context. Do not create diagnosis or treatment content.
